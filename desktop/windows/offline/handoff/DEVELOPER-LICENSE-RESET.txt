KAZMION POS 1.0.1 - DEVELOPER LICENCE AND RESET
Pilot candidate. Account recovery requires a verified delivery channel.

Developer access
1. On a new installation, sign in through Developer Engine using the configured
   platform developer email. The initial password setup uses the installation's
   setup token. The offline launcher generates it in the protected .env under
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\KazmiOn\OfflinePOS.
2. Use the token only for the authorized local setup. Keep it and the .env
   private. Record the developer account owner in your access register. Never
   include setup tokens, passwords, or JWT secrets in tickets or screenshots.

Business licence
1. In Developer Engine, open Business Tenants, locate the correct business, and
   open its details. Confirm the tenant ID independently of its display name.
2. In the deployment and licence section, set the approved deployment mode,
   branch limit, and expiry, then choose Save deployment & license. Enter an
   explicit renewal date to change the existing expiry.
3. The date picker is saved as an ISO date at 00:00 UTC. At that instant the
   licence expires. Confirm the corresponding local time before applying it.
4. Verify that the intended business can sign in after an authorized renewal,
   or that an intentionally expired pilot business cannot sign in, refresh a
   session, or open protected business operations. Confirm a second tenant is
   unaffected. Record the business ID, old and new expiry and approval reference
   without recording session tokens. Cloud sync stays disabled in V1.

Client password reset
1. Verify the requester and the target business through your support procedure.
   In Developer Engine, open the business's client account list, select the
   correct account, and choose Reset client password.
2. The account must be active and have a verified email. Select Send reset link.
   The application sends a single-use link to that verified address, with a
   15-minute validity window. The account holder chooses the new password.
3. Verify that the holder can sign in and that prior sessions require sign-in
   again. A used, expired, or superseded code or link should be rejected.
   Avoid asking the account holder to forward the link or verification code.

Offline delivery
Recovery links need a working approved mail relay and a reset URL reachable by
the recipient. An air-gapped pilot without a mail relay cannot deliver these
links. Record the unavailable channel and escalate through the approved
account-recovery process. Tenant licence changes and normal local sign-in can
operate offline. Do not edit account hashes or expiry fields directly to work
around access checks.
