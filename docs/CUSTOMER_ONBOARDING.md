# Cloud customer onboarding

Onboard customers after staging verifies isolation, account activation, subscriptions, password recovery, and restore. Give each customer its approved HTTPS portal URL and a verified support contact. The portal runs in a browser.

Confirm business name, verified owner contact, subscription terms, branch and staff limits, and any requested import. Provision and verify the business database, then issue an expiring activation link to the owner. The owner assigns individual staff accounts and branch permissions. Never collect or share staff passwords.

For a V1 migration, obtain authorization to use an encrypted export. Keep V1 available during reconciliation and have the owner verify opening balances, inventory, installment schedules, and representative receipts before accepting cutover. Record the approved import and retention dates. See [migration](MIGRATION.md).
